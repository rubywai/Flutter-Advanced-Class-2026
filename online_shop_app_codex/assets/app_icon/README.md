# Shopping App Icon

Master: `shop_icon.png` (1024 × 1024).
Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool; native sizes exported with macOS sips.

Prompt:
Create a production-ready square mobile app icon for an online shopping app.
A single bold white shopping bag symbol with a friendly subtle smile and rounded
handle, centered on a rich emerald green background with a very subtle green
gradient. Clean modern minimal geometric design, exceptional legibility at small
sizes, refined flat design, no text, no letters, no watermark, no surrounding
mockup. Full bleed opaque square artwork, no rounded outer corners or outer
border (the operating system applies its mask). Keep all parts of the white
shopping bag within the central 60 percent of the canvas to stay safe in circular
Android masks. 1024x1024 square.
